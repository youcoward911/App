# Scroll Pig - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 8, 2026**

## Overview
Scroll Pig ("the App") is a screen time management app. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data We Collect
- **Anonymous Authentication**: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a unique user ID. No personal information (name, email, phone) is collected.
- **Location (optional)**: If you grant permission, we access your city-level location solely to display local leaderboard rankings. Your precise location is never stored.
- **Usage Data**: App lock/unlock counts and coin totals are stored to provide leaderboard functionality.

## How We Use Your Data
- To display your stats and leaderboard rankings
- To sync your progress across app sessions
- We do **not** sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties
- We do **not** track you for advertising purposes

## Data Storage
Data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore). Anonymous user IDs cannot be linked to your real identity.

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication and database — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Expo**: Build and update infrastructure — [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.
